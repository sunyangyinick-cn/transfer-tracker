What this app is for

Follow the summer 2026 transfer window for Premier League clubs. Every real-world report links to its source, so you can check it yourself.

Start by searching for a player or club, or choose a league and then a club.

Two kinds of card, each labelled in words:

  • Real-world report A move reported by a club or by Transfermarkt, with a solid border. Its status says how far it is confirmed: Official means the club announced it; Transfermarkt record means a transfer database lists it but no club announcement was found.
  • Fan wish · not news Players you would like a club to sign, with a dotted border, kept on the Fan wishlist tab.

Club crests are the clubs' trademarks, shown only to identify them; no club endorses this app. The images load from Wikipedia and Wikimedia Commons, and each crest links to its file page, which records its copyright and licence. Most are non-free files that Wikipedia uses under its own fair-use rationale, which isn't permission for this app. A dashed "no crest" box means no crest file was found or the image didn't load.
